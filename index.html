Our Story

That's why we started ShogunShip—not to get rich, but to share the thrill of Japanese collecting with people like you.

Questions?

We've got answers

How long does the whole process take? ⌄
Finding an item typically takes 1-2 weeks depending on what you're looking for and current marketplace activity. Once purchased, items arrive at our storage within a few days. Storage time depends on you—consolidate as many items as you want, then ship when ready. International shipping takes 1-3 weeks depending on destination and service chosen.
Can I request custom searches? ⌄
Absolutely. That's what we do. Send us links, item descriptions, specific criteria—anything. We'll search Japanese marketplaces and find exactly what you're looking for. The thrill of the hunt is part of why we started ShogunShip.
What if I don't like the item when it arrives? ⌄
We send arrival photos before international shipping, so you'll see exactly what you're getting. But we understand concerns about condition or quality expectations. Contact us immediately if there's an issue—we'll work with you to resolve it.
Do you handle rare or high-value items? ⌄
Yes. We've handled everything from vintage collectibles to limited edition figures. The rarer and more specialized your request, the more valuable our expertise becomes. We understand Japanese collector markets and can navigate authentication, condition assessment, and safe handling of high-value pieces.
How is international shipping handled? ⌄
We partner with international carriers like EMS and DHL. All shipments include full insurance and tracking. Items are carefully packed. You can consolidate multiple purchases before shipping to save on costs. We'll handle all customs documentation.
What about customs and import taxes? ⌄
Customs and import taxes vary by destination country. We'll provide accurate item valuations to declare. You may owe taxes on delivery depending on your country's import regulations. We'll give you realistic expectations about potential additional costs.
Can you help with items on specific platforms? ⌄
We shop from all major Japanese marketplaces—Mercari, Yahoo Auctions, Rakuma, Mandarake, Surugaya, Rakuten, and others. Have a specific platform in mind? Tell us, and we'll find what you're looking for there.
How do you handle payment? ⌄
Reach out with your request. We'll provide a quote including the item price plus our 15% service fee. Payment methods and details will be discussed directly. We keep everything transparent and straightforward.
